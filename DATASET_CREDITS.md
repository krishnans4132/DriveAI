# Dataset Credits and Usage Notice

DriveAlert AI uses locally obtained data from the following third-party datasets for research and educational experiments. The original videos and extracted image datasets are **not distributed in this repository**.

## UTA Real-Life Drowsiness Dataset (UTA-RLDD)

- Official dataset page: [UTA-RLDD](https://sites.google.com/view/utarldd/home)
- Associated paper: R. Ghoddoosian, M. Galib, and V. Athitsos, “A Realistic Dataset and Baseline Temporal Model for Early Drowsiness Detection,” *2019 IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Workshops (CVPRW)*, 2019. [arXiv:1904.07312](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07312)

The official UTA-RLDD page asks publications, presentations, and posters reporting results from the dataset to cite the associated paper. It also describes participant privacy restrictions. Obtain the dataset from its official page and follow the access, privacy, and usage conditions supplied by its maintainers.

## Yawning Detection Dataset (YawDD)

- Official dataset record: [YawDD on IEEE DataPort](https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/yawdd-yawning-detection-dataset)
- Dataset DOI: [10.21227/e1qm-hb90](https://doi.org/10.21227/e1qm-hb90)
- Associated paper: S. Abtahi, M. Omidyeganeh, S. Shirmohammadi, and B. Hariri, “YawDD: A Yawning Detection Dataset,” *Proceedings of the 5th ACM Multimedia Systems Conference*, pp. 24–28, 2014. [doi:10.1145/2557642.2563678](https://doi.org/10.1145/2557642.2563678)

Obtain YawDD from its official IEEE DataPort record and comply with the terms shown there.
